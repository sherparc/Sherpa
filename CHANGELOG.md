# Changelog

Every entry is a merge into `main` — the squash commit's own message, written by the author of the change —
laid out per release by [`scripts/changelog.py`](scripts/changelog.py); nothing here is edited by hand.
Releases are the tags `v<version>`; the wheel of each is on [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/sherparc/) and
attached to the [GitHub release](https://github.com/sherparc/Sherpa/releases).
